# Evidence — F1: Growth-funded ownership, contingent on employment

**Claim (register):** A defined share of incremental federal receipts from AI-driven growth could fund
broad ownership — *if employment holds or grows.*
**Status: `open`** — the conditional is the claim; whether the condition holds is genuinely unresolved.
**Warrant (proposed):** because ~15 of the ~17.8 federal-receipts points ride on wages, incremental
receipts scale with the employment regime — growth converts into fundable receipts only where employment
holds or grows.

## Derivation
Net incremental receipts are decomposed by where the new income lands:

`R = dGDP·[λ·τ_L + (1−λ)·τ_K·(1−foreign)·realiz] + d_emp·wage_base·GDP`

(λ = marginal labor share of new GDP; τ_L ≈ 0.26 on wages vs τ_K ≈ 0.15 on domestic *realized* capital;
foreign ≈ 18% equity-income leakage — record high; wage_base ≈ 13.2% of GDP.) Three employment regimes
were run against a literature-anchored GDP-uplift ladder — Acemoglu (~1%, the credentialed bear floor),
PWBM (1.5%), mid (3%), Goldman (7%):
- **Stable employment** reproduces the naïve 17.8% take (sanity check) — the mechanism funds.
- **Reinstatement** (new tasks favor labor) out-earns naïve (~1.07×).
- **Displacement** starves it: receipts per GDP-dollar fall ~30%, and the *level* effect of a shrinking
  wage base turns net receipts **negative** at low-growth scenarios — lost wage taxes exceed new capital
  taxes. The regimes map onto Acemoglu-Restrepo's displacement-vs-reinstatement task framework; the
  stable-λ (0.55) matches the measured (record-low, falling) labor share.
Model: `ai_growth_receipts_gate.py` (pure computation; the Item-4 scenario runner reproduces this table).

## Sensitivity
d_emp (the employment-level dial, ±2%) is the contested parameter and dominates at low growth; λ next;
τ_K/realiz/foreign matter secondarily. The cautionary base rate: the labor share has trended *toward*
displacement for decades.

## Honest limits (why `open`)
The decomposition is structural, not estimated: no empirical receipts-elasticity under AI-specific
displacement exists yet (the register's disconfirming test invites exactly that). WS-4 added a further
caution: the AI-*attributable* receipts signal may sit inside CBO's own forecast error (see F3) — even a
true positive R may not be *identifiable* enough to draw against honestly.

## Disconfirming test (register)
A receipts-elasticity estimate showing an extra point of AI-driven GDP yields ~zero or negative *net*
federal receipts under realistic displacement/reinstatement dynamics.
