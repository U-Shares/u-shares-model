# Evidence — F2: The realized-only draw rule (designed neutrality, not validated)

**Claim (register):** The credit spend is *designed* to be deficit-neutral — drawing only on realized
above-baseline receipts, never borrowing — a modeling aspiration, not yet validated.
**Status: `open`.**
**Warrant (proposed):** a rule that spends only *realized* above-baseline receipts and never borrows
cannot mechanically worsen the baseline in the spend year; cycle-neutrality then turns entirely on
reserving for the standing deductibility cost.

## Derivation
1. **The spend-year mechanics.** Budget_t = s × max(R_t, 0), spent only after realization. The undedicated
   (1−s) share reduces the deficit; under displacement R_t ≤ 0 and the program correctly self-limits to
   zero. In this narrow, spend-year sense the rule is safe *by construction*. Model: `deficit_guardrail.py`
   (account dynamics, placement pausing in receipts dropouts, capacity S_max = s·R/c).
2. **Why that is NOT deficit-neutrality (the honest core of the file).** Two standing costs run outside
   the spend-year logic: (a) the dividend's **deductibility** (~1.05%/yr of placed stock) continues in
   recessions when the budget is zero — without a pre-funded reserve it becomes a recession deficit draw
   (the guardrail run shows the account going negative in a modeled 2-yr dropout absent the reserve);
   (b) a **Treasuries-invested reserve liquidated in a claim year is, consolidated, borrowing in the draw
   state** (WS-4). Neutrality over the *cycle* therefore requires the reserve (F6) and an honest
   accounting of its draw-state cost — neither validated.
3. **Timing/debt-service mechanics** (realized-vs-projected lags, interest on within-year timing) are
   specified in the model's structure but not scored.

## Sensitivity
The self-limiting property is robust to s; the recession draw scales with placed stock × c; baseline
integrity (F3) is a strict prerequisite — a gamed baseline converts the rule into ordinary spending.

## Disconfirming test (register)
A worked deficit-guardrail (debt-service + timing) in which the reserve is empty under displacement, or
neutrality breaks.
