# Evidence — F5: Most firms clear the general issuance bar across the cycle

**Claim (register):** The ~3.95% after-tax general issuance bar is cleared by most firms (77–99%,
firm-weighted, 2007–2025).
**Status: `supported`** (measured, with proxy caveats stated in full).
**Warrant (proposed):** public industry-return data across two recessions shows the share of the
corporate universe out-earning the after-tax cost of the deductible 5% coupon never fell below roughly
three-quarters, firm-weighted — so the issuance economics of F4 hold not just on average but through the
cycle's troughs.

## Measurement
Damodaran (NYU Stern) industry EVA datasets, archived year-snapshots 2007, 2008, 2009, 2018, 2019, 2020,
2025 — spanning the GFC and COVID recessions. Firm-weighted share of the universe with industry ROIC ≥
the bar. Harness: `claim3b_cyclical_stress.py` (re-run 2026-06-21).

| Year | ≥3.95% (general) | ≥5% (floor) | ≥7.58% (discount-to-poverty) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2007 | 99.1% | 95.6% | 88.8% |
| 2008 | 98.7% | 98.0% | 89.9% |
| 2009 (GFC trough) | 97.2% | 97.2% | 81.8% |
| 2018 | 82.7% | 81.4% | 75.0% |
| 2019 | 82.5% | 82.5% | 77.3% |
| 2020 (COVID) | 76.9% | 76.2% | 58.4% |
| 2025 | 84.3% | 84.2% | 79.7% |

**Read the within-episode drops, not the cross-year levels:** GFC 2007→09, general bar −1.9pp; COVID
2019→20, −5.6pp. The discount-to-poverty bar is ~3× more recession-fragile (−18.9pp through COVID) —
which is why the architecture never asks a firm to clear it (F4).

## Caveats (the honest limits of the measurement)
1. **Industry-level, not firm-level:** within a clearing industry some firms fall short (and vice versa);
   clearance is a supply *proxy*, not a firm census.
2. **ROIC ≠ ROA:** Damodaran publishes ROIC; true ROA runs somewhat lower, so clearance is modestly
   optimistic. The claim's own replication ask (OQ-FIS-005) invites the true-ROA re-measurement.
3. **Survivorship** (~1–2 pp, listed-universe bias) and **composition noise** (the universe expanded
   post-2015 toward loss-making small/micro-caps — the level shift between 2009 and 2018 is partly
   composition, not profitability).

## Disconfirming test (register)
Firm-level returns using true ROA (vs ROIC), or cycle extension, showing far fewer firms clear — or the
F4 adoption test failing despite favorable economics.
