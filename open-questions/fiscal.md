# Open Questions — Fiscal Hawk (OQ-FIS)

**Persona.** Cost, durability, sizing, and the employment-contingency of the funding path — does the
growth-funded credit actually stay affordable and deficit-neutral, and is the safety reserve sized right?
**How to engage.** Claim a question via Issue (`question-claim.yml`) citing its OQ-ID; falsification is
typically a **scenario run** on documented assumptions. Claim links point to `CLAIMS.md`.
*(Maintainer-written, contributor-read.)*

---

### OQ-FIS-001 — Receipts elasticity under labor displacement
- **Question.** Most federal revenue rides on wages. Under realistic displacement/reinstatement dynamics
  (Acemoglu–Restrepo vs. the reinstatement view), how much federal receipt does an extra point of
  AI-driven GDP actually generate?
- **What an answer would look like.** An estimate of receipts per point of AI GDP as a function of the
  employment/labor-share dials, submitted as a **scenario file** (an employment regime) that confirms or
  flips F1's disconfirming test.
- **Links to claim.** F1 (AI growth can fund broad ownership — conditional on employment).
- **Route.** Falsification.

### OQ-FIS-002 — The honest baseline
- **Question.** "Draw only on receipts above a pre-AI baseline" is only as sound as the baseline. How
  should a non-manipulable counterfactual baseline be defined and governed?
- **What an answer would look like.** A specified, reproducible baseline methodology plus a governance
  rule preventing gaming, demonstrated on historical data.
- **Links to claim.** F1 (funding path) · F3 (deficit-neutral-above-baseline — open / modeling aspiration).
- **Route.** Falsification / extension (a method proposal is an extension; a demonstrated flaw falsifies).

### OQ-FIS-003 — Deductibility cost, net of holder mix
- **Question.** The 5% dividend is corporate-deductible. What is its *true permanent* fiscal cost once
  holder-side taxation and recapture are accounted for?
- **What an answer would look like.** An estimate of the net ongoing cost rate (c) by holder tier, feeding
  the capacity relation S_max = s·R/c.
- **Links to claim.** F2 (credit spend deficit-neutrality — a modeling aspiration — conditional on the reserve).
- **Route.** Falsification.

### OQ-FIS-004 — Backstop reserve sizing
- **Question.** Is the safety-side reserve for the fixed-indexed floor sized correctly under stress?
- **What an answer would look like.** An independent ALM/ruin computation (regime-switching, negative
  inflation/return covariance) checking the order-of-magnitude reserve claim.
- **Links to claim.** F6 (safety side is one affordable reserve) · GV1 (governance/durability).
- **Route.** Falsification.

### OQ-FIS-005 — Independent ROA / break-even replication
- **Question.** Do the 3.95% / 7.58% break-even bars survive independent replication?
- **What an answer would look like.** Firm- and industry-level replication of the 3.95% / 7.58% bars
  across the cycle (recession troughs and sector heterogeneity), using public data.
- **Links to claim.** F4 (issuance not the binding constraint) · F5 (general bar cleared across the cycle).
- **Route.** Falsification (empirical).

### OQ-FIS-006 — State-level fiscal note (Kansas test case)
- **Question.** What would a state-level fiscal note — Kansas as the test case — show?
- **What an answer would look like.** Single-state modeling of what a state can legitimately count that
  the federal CBO cannot.
- **Links to claim.** F1 (funding path).
- **Route.** Extension.

### OQ-FIS-007 — Transferable-credit market design
- **Question.** Why does the §6418 market clear near $0.95/$1 while LIHTC runs 10–27% leakage?
- **What an answer would look like.** A design that replicates the former, a numeric ceiling on any
  recycling multiplier (IRC §38(c)), and a sober assessment of any unrealized-appreciation credit's
  viability.
- **Links to claim.** F2 (credit spend deficit-neutrality — a modeling aspiration) · F4 (issuance not the binding
  constraint).
- **Route.** Extension.

### OQ-FIS-008 — Legal/tax classification: equity vs. debt
- **Question.** Can a fixed, inflation-indexed, enforceable 5% floor be structured as a genuine ownership
  interest (equity), not debt?
- **What an answer would look like.** A tax/securities counsel opinion on whether an enforceable fixed 5%
  floor preserves equity (not debt) treatment across tax, securities, bankruptcy, and property law.
- **Links to claim.** L1 (equity-not-debt classification).
- **Route.** Falsification (a counsel opinion that the floor forces debt treatment falsifies L1).

### OQ-FIS-009 — Net fiscal impact model (20–30 yr)
- **Question.** What is the program's net fiscal impact over a 20–30 year horizon — static and dynamic?
- **What an answer would look like.** A projection incorporating the proposal's own figures, a safety-net
  offset calibrated against Alaska PFD / EITC evidence, and the gap between a CBO-style static score and
  a dynamic one.
- **Links to claim.** F1 (funding path) · F2 (credit spend deficit-neutrality — a modeling aspiration) · M0
  (magnitudes held).
- **Route.** Extension (the flagship modeling ask).

### OQ-FIS-010 — The magnitudes
- **Question.** Reach per person, receipts, credit capacity, the exact reserve — all deliberately held.
  Can any of them be established or overturned?
- **What an answer would look like.** Validated, credentialed-grade (G3) modeling that establishes or
  overturns any of these numbers.
- **Links to claim.** F7 (poverty-first reach — magnitudes open) · M0 (the standing magnitudes gate).
- **Route.** Falsification (these remain unasserted pending validation).
