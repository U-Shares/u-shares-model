# Open Questions — Adjacent-Policy Expert (OQ-AP)

**Persona.** Honest positioning against — and interaction with — existing vehicles: UBI, baby bonds,
ESOPs, 401(k)s, sovereign wealth funds, and Stout-style citizen capitalism. Where does U-Shares dominate,
where does it merely complement, and where does it lose?
**How to engage.** Claim via Issue (`question-claim.yml`) citing the OQ-ID. Claim links → `CLAIMS.md`.
*(Maintainer-written, contributor-read.)*

---

### OQ-AP-001 — Honest positioning vs. the ownership-broadening family
- **Question.** On which dimensions does U-Shares dominate, complement, or lose to baby bonds, ESOPs, UBI,
  **Stout-style citizen capitalism** (issuance-not-donation; individual, heritable ownership;
  poverty-first), and Diamandis's **MOSAIC / UHI** (ownership vs. transfer) — and what is the honest
  comparative case?
- **What an answer would look like.** A structured comparison across stated dimensions (dispersion power,
  fiscal cost, durability, political feasibility, stickiness) with an explicit account of where U-Shares
  is *worse*, not only where it is better — where it complements, where it differs, where it competes.
- **Links to claim.** CP1 (comparative positioning).
- **Route.** Extension (comparative framing — not a falsification path).

### OQ-AP-002 — Interaction / crowd-out with existing structures
- **Question.** Does U-Shares complement or crowd out 401(k) / IRA / ESOP structures and a prospective

- **What an answer would look like.** An analysis of substitution/complementarity with existing
  ownership and retirement vehicles and the household balance sheet, including any displacement of
  existing saving.
- **Links to claim.** CP1 (comparative positioning — interaction facet) · F7 (poverty-first reach).
- **Route.** Extension.

### OQ-AP-003 — Can a placement credit legally direct corporate equity into a §530A account?
- **Question.** Trump Accounts (§530A) restrict investments to low-cost broad-index funds and cap
  contributions ($5,000/yr; the Dell pledge shows third-party funding is workable). Can a U-Share
  placement credit lawfully deliver *corporate-issued equity* (or fund-wrapped U-Shares) into a §530A
  account — and what statutory or regulatory changes would that take?
- **What an answer would look like.** A legal/administrative analysis of §530A's investment and
  contribution rules (statute + Treasury/IRS regs) mapping the minimal amendments for U-Share placement —
  direct, or via an index-fund-wrapped U-Share vehicle — with the contribution-cap and election-mechanics
  interactions worked.
- **Links to claim.** CP1 (the rails thesis) · F4 (placement-credit architecture).
- **Route.** Extension (legal/design analysis).

### OQ-AP-004 — Can the insured 5% floor attach to holdings inside a §530A account?
- **Question.** The U-Share floor (mandatory dividend + PBGC-style guaranty with issuer recourse) is the
  family's only downside protection. Can it attach to instruments held in a §530A wrapper — and does the
  guaranty entity's recourse survive the account's tax treatment and the holder's minority?
- **What an answer would look like.** A design note reconciling the floor/guaranty mechanics with §530A's
  IRA-like treatment (income within a tax-deferred wrapper, claim payment routing, recourse against the
  issuer, treatment at the age-18 conversion).
- **Links to claim.** CP1 · F6 (the combined reserve).
- **Route.** Extension (design analysis).

### OQ-AP-005 — The eligibility-expansion path (and the age-18 boundary)
- **Question.** The §530A seed is cohort-limited (born 2025–2028) and the account architecture is
  child-only, converting to ordinary IRA treatment at 18 — exactly where the SSI disregard also lapses
  (OQ-HH-004). What is the realistic legislative path to extend seeded, protected, disregarded accounts
  beyond the cohort and past 18 — poverty-first?
- **What an answer would look like.** A legislative-design analysis: which §530A parameters (cohort,
  seed size, age boundary, disregard) can be extended by amendment vs. requiring the fuller U-Shares
  architecture; sequencing; and cost mechanics per tier (magnitudes held, M0).
- **Links to claim.** CP1 · F7 (poverty-first ladder) · RT1 (the adult wrapper).
- **Route.** Extension (legislative design).

### OQ-AP-006 — Does the corporate form persist long enough to matter? *(added 2026-07-22 — *The Last Economy* study)*
- **Question.** U-Shares' funding engine assumes corporations durable enough to issue against future
  earnings. *The Last Economy* (Intelligent Internet, 2025) argues both sides itself: Ch. 11 forecasts
  the firm dissolving into AI-native networks; Ch. 20 recasts corporations as durable, evolving "slow
  AI." If firm dissolution wins, the issuance counterparty thins — a shared exposure across every
  issuance-based member of this family. Which reading does the evidence favor, on what horizon?
- **What an answer would look like.** Institutional-economics or empirical work on firm-form durability
  under AI adoption, translated into issuance-capacity terms.
- **Links to claim.** F4/F5 (issuance) · CP1 (family-wide dependence on issuers/employers).
- **Route.** Argument + evidence (political-economy adjacent).
